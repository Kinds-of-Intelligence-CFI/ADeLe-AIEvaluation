## 2026-10-25 — one driver: what the task needs from the other party (ms-candidate, v4b)

OPERATIVE CHANGE, at Pablo's direction. Framework (Pablo, 2026-10-17): the demand is how hard it is to get from the
other party what the task needs. A task needs something from them only if its success depends on it, as the task
states it or as it is judged; the kind of obstacle sets the level, amounts do not (0 no one; 1 nothing in the way,
including a request turned down; 2 a willing party guided; 3 doubts to be overcome; 4 opposition to be overcome;
5 parties whose needs cannot all be met). New intro and level statements in MSm's style; the Level 4 refund example
states the needed stance ("…to accept the store credit it does offer"). The "does not cover" paragraph is unchanged.
Why: the old levels graded the counterpart's disposition, so judges put every refusal of a persistent party at 4,
reading the refusal as something the party must accept (msc-l4-note; ms-candidate amendments 1 to 3).
Measured (experiments/benchmarks/ms-candidate, Opus 5.5 low, three repeats, reasoning and bare-digit prompts): every
MSc lab check holds (battery M01 rises to 4; r36 D2 and battery M04 noisy, losses not confirmed in pass 2); the
refusal pairs score 1 except the gym pair (4); the curfew, supplier and builder pairs, which need the party's
agreement, score 4; on 15 tau2 tasks labelled by Pablo from the agent's side, 13 exact under both prompts (current
text 6), 15 of 15 against his two post-discussion revisions. Bare digit against reasoning: 98% exact.
