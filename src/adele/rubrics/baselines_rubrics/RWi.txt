This rubric evaluates whether a task requires finding and delivering relevant factual or background information in response to queries, often based on pre-trained knowledge or retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). This capability focuses on accessing and providing existing information rather than creating new content.

Level 0 None. The task does not require retrieving, finding, or providing specific factual information or background knowledge. No information lookup or fact-finding is needed. Examples: 
* "Create a fictional backstory for Marie Curie" 
* "Write a personal opinion about modern art" 
* "Generate a random password" 

Level 1 Present. The task requires finding and delivering relevant factual or background information in response to specific queries. This includes accessing knowledge databases, providing current information, and retrieving specific facts or data points. Examples: 
* "Looking at several supermarkets online, what is the current price of mangoes in Australia" 
* "Who won the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2023?" 
* "What are the symptoms of Type 2 diabetes?"
