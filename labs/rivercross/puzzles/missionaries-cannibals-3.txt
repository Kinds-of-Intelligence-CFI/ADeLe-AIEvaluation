On an unattended bank, the C1/C2/C3 must never outnumber the M1/M2/M3 when any of the latter are present. Find a sequence of crossings that gets everything to the right bank.

Present ONLY your solution as a numbered list, one crossing per line, naming the passengers in the boat on that trip using the exact item names. For example:
