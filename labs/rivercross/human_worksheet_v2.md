## Rubric cheat-sheets (0-5)

**QLl — Logical Reasoning** (deductive reasoning to find valid moves / finish):
- 0 none (recall only) · 1 apply ONE rule / single implication (even if multi-step) · 2 combine TWO distinct premises (basic syllogism) · 3 integrate MULTIPLE INTERACTING premises, negations/quantifiers · 4 long chains, many premises, systematic case-tracking · 5 abstract/nested, evaluate hypotheses, find fallacies.
- *Binding note:* count only premises that actually bind; conditions trivially satisfied (e.g. ample boat slack) add no demand.

**MCt — Critical Thinking Processes** (monitoring/regulating multiple thought processes):
- 0 none · 1 recall/recognition · 2 straightforward comprehension/application of known concepts, simple comparisons · 3 considerable analysis/synthesis, identify patterns, make inferences · 4 evaluate complex ideas, multiple perspectives/assumptions, biases · 5 sophisticated metacognition, fallacies, competing arguments.
