## 3. Provenance entry written

`docs/taxonomy-provenance.md` gains a PLs section plus a correction notice. Two entries in
that document had gone stale and are now annotated rather than rewritten: PLe is no longer
keyed to irreversibility, and the `MSe`→`PLs` row refers to the *deleted* dimension, not this
one.

The grounding, per Pablo: PLs is keyed to the capacity mammals evolved for running a
situation forward internally — prospection. The entry records why that makes it a dimension
rather than a task feature (it recurs as a mechanism across unrelated behaviours, and is
studied under several names for the same operation), and the two consequences written into
the rubric: what is simulated is what the world does rather than what to do about it, and the
demand scales with interacting change at the required precision, not with how specialised the
regularities are. It also notes that §10 of the artifact proposed "a causal / intuitive-physics
dimension" and marked it out of scope — PLs arguably answers that proposal in a broader,
modality-general form.
