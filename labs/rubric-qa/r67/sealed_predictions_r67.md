# r67 sealed — genre-free repair of the L5 misroute, relabel of the r66 sample (2026-08-26)

## The change (v19), before any relabelling

Pablo rejected the forecasting-sentence fix: it names a task class, which no operative clause in
the family does. The deeper defect: L5's "a model good enough to settle the question cannot be
had" misroutes any irreducibly uncertain outcome, since for a stock price nothing settles the
question. Repair is to define adequacy relative to the best achievable answer, in three genre-free
edits:

1. Preamble, added after the rule clause: a rule that answers the question **as well as it can be
   answered** supplies the model too, so a task settled by a standing rate stays low however
   uncertain the outcome remains.
2. L5: the forecasting sentence is deleted outright.
3. L5, new Note: an outcome no one can call does not by itself place a task here; where nothing
   does better than a standing rate, the rate supplies the model and makes only its demand; what
   belongs here is a situation whose own workings could support a better answer, though no model
   of them can be trusted to give it.

The bank run stays at 5 under (3): its described mechanism carries structure a rate ignores, and no
model can be trusted to exploit it. The stock does not: the rate ignores nothing.
