The two that needed real scrutiny, and why they stand:

- **S6, height from a shadow = 5.** The height is genuinely not depicted; it follows only by
  inverting the scene's formation (sun elevation, projection), with the sun angle supplied from
  beyond the image — the driver at its maximum, same class as the shading-to-relief example.
- **A5, table thickness from a dropped coin = 5.** The carried-by-the-signal doctrine says the ring
  of a struck object is carried (the L1 struck-material example), so why is this 5? The line the
  rubric draws is between content recognisable by acquaintance (a material's ring, heard as such)
  and content recoverable only by modelling the source and working back (modal frequencies to plate
  thickness). L5's wording carries this: the signal "does not carry the content AS CONTENT."
  Same class as the string length-and-tension example. The distinction is coherent and the judges
  applied it 5/5/5.
