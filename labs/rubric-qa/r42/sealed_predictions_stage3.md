## Pre-registered decision rule

1. **Diagonal holds**: every item ≥3 on its own dimension. Failure means the item was
   mis-designed, and that item is dropped from the leakage analysis rather than reinterpreted.
2. **Off-diagonal**: any cell ≥3 on a dimension the item was not built for is a LEAK and
   names the pair needing text work. A cell exactly 2 levels above its prediction is recorded
   as a soft leak.
3. **The PLp/PLs verdict** rests on PLp-3 versus PLp-1/PLp-2, as above.
4. Seed spread ≥2 in a cell → unstable, excluded from claims, reported.
