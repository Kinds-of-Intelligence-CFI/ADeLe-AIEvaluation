## The epistemic distinction at stake, stated before measuring

Difficulty in "finding a base rate" splits three ways. A rate that is catalogued but obscure is
knowledge or search, not simulating (the rubric's own carve). A rate that no one has catalogued
and that must be constructed from the situation's workings — regime cohesion, board dynamics,
disclosure processes — requires modelling those workings, which is simulating, and the v19 clause
should NOT fire because such a rate is not "standing". Conflicting catalogued rates needing
adjudication sit between and are not this round's business. If single-call judges route
constructed-rate items to 3+, the text already draws this line and only batch labelling was at
fault. If they hold them at 2 while Pablo would say 3-4, the clause over-fires and that is the
measured wrong score a text guard would need.
