## Pre-registered decision rules

1. **The spread claim**: among B1 to B8, at least three distinct levels appear and at least two
   items reach 4 or above. This is the round's purpose. If the whole set lands at 2 or 3, PLs is
   genuinely narrow, the forecasting slice does not rescue it, and that goes into the provenance
   entry as a finding rather than being explained away.
2. **Forecasting does not over-fire**: B1 ≤ 2 and B7 ≤ 2.
3. **Executability**: A2 − A3 ≥ 2, and A4 ≥ 3.
4. **The second Note holds**: A1 = 4.
5. **Overall**: ≥ 9 of 12 within 1 of prediction.

Rule 1 is reported whatever it says. A failure of rule 2 sends the Level 5 forecasting sentence
back to text. A failure of rule 3 means the widened clause is unmeasured, not validated.
